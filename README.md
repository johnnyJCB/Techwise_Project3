🎉 New Features: Keyword Insights & Readability Score
🚀 Overview

Say hello to smarter text analysis! The latest VibeChecker update brings Keyword Insights and Readability Score right to your fingertips—no backend API needed. Instantly understand your text, and communicate like a pro.

📝 Keyword Insights Tab

🎯 Purpose:
Discover the words that define your message! Highlight the top themes and see which keywords are driving your content.

⚡ How It Works:

Type or paste any text.

Click “Analyze Keywords”.

Voilà! See the top most used words with their counts.

🔧 Technical Magic:

Text is cleaned up with punctuation removal, normalization, and stopword filtering.

💡 Why You’ll Love It:

Quickly spot recurring themes.

Understand what makes your message tick.

Make your writing sharper and more focused.

📚 Readability Score Tab

🎯 Purpose:
Find out how easy (or tricky!) your text is to read. Perfect for tailoring your writing to any audience.

⚡ How It Works:

Enter your text.

Click “Analyze Readability”.

Get a Flesch–Kincaid-style readability score and an easy-to-understand grade-level interpretation.

🔧 Technical Magic:

Scores range from “Very easy (middle school)” to “Extremely difficult/academic”.


💡 Why You’ll Love It:

Make sure your message hits the right reading level.

Engage your audience with clarity.

Perfect for social posts, emails, reports—you name it!
